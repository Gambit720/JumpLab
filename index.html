How to use, assumptions, application, credits

Instructions: enter values and press Apply inputs, press Start, then press Space or the Up arrow to jump. Hit an obstacle and the run ends. Your score is the horizontal distance in metres.

Assumptions: constant gravitational acceleration during a jump, no air resistance, jump starts from the ground with vertical speed v₀, motion in a straight line. The animation uses a declared scale (see the note under the game); labels and numbers use real SI units.

Real-life application: a jump is projectile motion. The same equations describe athletes, vehicles launching off ramps, and why a person jumps much higher on the Moon.
